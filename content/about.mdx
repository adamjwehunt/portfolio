## Biography

Born in [Mesa, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesa,_Arizona 'Mesa, Arizona Wiki'), I found my love for computers, programming, and web design during my high school years, usually with a half-built PC on my desk. In college, I created websites in my free time and earned an associate's degree. After graduating, I felt a calling to work outdoors ☀️ and do hands-on work. So I led a team installing solar panels across the valley. It was fun, meaningful work, and it let me make a positive impact on the environment 🌱.

Eventually, I went all in on my passion and joined a coding bootcamp 🪖 to jumpstart my skills at building modern web apps. I then joined [Faithlife](https://www.faithlife.com), a huge Bible software company, as a Full Stack Engineer. That's where my software career really began. I worked on websites, web apps, and desktop applications, plus TV apps for Roku, Apple TV, Fire TV, and more.

After Faithlife, I took a break to travel and share quality time with my wife and daughter. We relocated as a family, and in 2023 I started working remotely at [MotorTrend](https://www.motortrend.com 'MotorTrend'), helping modernize the site with React and Next.js. That work carried on into Hearst Autos, where I build across MotorTrend and [Car and Driver](https://www.caranddriver.com 'Car and Driver'): major platform migrations, CMS improvements, and car shopping and discovery experiences 🚗.

## Day-to-day

These days, we live in Washington, just outside of Portland 🌲🌲🌲. Coming from the desert, I still can't get over how green everything is.

When away from the screen, I love taking a step outside. Whether it's a hike through the forest or a family adventure, I find comfort in nature and feel energized by the calm 🧘 it gives. And when we're not exploring, you'll find us playing board games, making something together, or doing whatever our imagination brings.

After TLO (the little one) goes to bed, I'm hanging out on the couch with my wife. She's an amazing person and artist (see her work [here](https://www.instagram.com/marisariveraart/ 'Marisa Rivera (@marisariveraart)')). I built [Marisa Rivera Art](https://marisariveraart.com 'Marisa Rivera Art'), her online shop, to help her sell her art and grow her business. I'm probably coding (lately, I've been planning out games for my daughter). Other times, painting miniatures for tabletop games 🧐🎨. For me, it's all about creating, learning something new, or simply having a good laugh.

My favorite part of all of it is taking an idea from "wouldn't it be cool if..." to a real, shipped thing that people actually use. 🎉

I value the freedom of working remote. I share more of the day with my family and feel closer to my community. It's a simple and fulfilling life that I am grateful to be living.
